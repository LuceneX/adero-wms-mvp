Questions for the first workshop

Use this screen to turn a visually compelling demo into a grounded product conversation.

Start with the operation

What does a “warehouse job” start with?Customer order, transport booking, ASN, phone call, or something else?

Which cargo actually enters storage?Dry, chilled, frozen, oversized, bonded, or mixed loads?

What has to be traceable?Lot, expiry, pallet, serial, temperature, customer ownership, or customs status?

Where do errors happen today?Receiving, stock visibility, picking, paperwork, carrier hand-off, or billing?

Define the real constraints

How many sites, users, and shifts?Include seasonal peaks and external customers who may need access.

Which devices are already used?Phones, rugged scanners, label printers, scales, temperature sensors?

What must integrate?Accounting, customs, transport management, customer portals, carriers, EDI/API?

What does ownership mean?Client-owned server, client-owned code, source escrow, or simply no per-user subscription?
