# ADERO discovery questions

Use these questions to replace assumptions with evidence. Capture the answer, the person who confirmed it, and an example record or document where possible.

## Business scope

1. Is ADERO looking for internal warehouse control, a customer-facing 3PL portal, or both?
2. Which warehouse sites and legal entities are in scope first?
3. What does “own system” mean: no recurring license, source-code ownership, deployment control, data residency, or all of these?
4. Which existing systems must remain the system of record?

## Inbound and storage

5. What starts an inbound job: transport order, supplier ASN, customer booking, email, or phone call?
6. How are goods identified: SKU, customer product code, barcode, pallet ID, lot, serial number?
7. Are receiving quantities counted by cases, pallets, kilograms, litres, or mixed units?
8. What inspections happen at receiving? Is temperature recorded, and what creates a hold?
9. Are locations fixed, directed, customer-dedicated, temperature-restricted, bonded, or dynamic?
10. Are goods owned by ADERO, a shipper, or multiple customers in the same facility?

## Outbound and services

11. What creates an outbound task, and how are priorities decided?
12. Is picking single-order, batch, wave, zone, pallet, or another method?
13. What does packing/assembly mean for ADERO’s actual services?
14. Which documents are required for dispatch, customs, temperature control, and proof of delivery?
15. Are carrier booking and tracking inside the WMS or in a transport system?

## People, devices, and scale

16. How many users, shifts, sites, customers, arrivals, order lines, and stock movements occur in a normal and peak month?
17. Which devices are used on the floor, and is Wi-Fi reliable throughout the warehouse?
18. Which printers, scales, scanners, sensors, and label formats are required?
19. Which roles must see or approve sensitive customer, customs, or pricing information?

## Delivery and ownership

20. What would make a 4–6 week pilot successful?
21. What data can be anonymized for the pilot, and who can approve it?
22. Who owns backups, security updates, upgrades, and incident response after launch?
23. What is the acceptable downtime and restore point for warehouse operations?
24. Which license and dependency obligations must the client’s lawyer or procurement team approve?
